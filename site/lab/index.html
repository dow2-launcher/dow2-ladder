Experiments

Add-ons and tools we are testing with the community. Easiest way: install the launcher and tick them in “Add-ons” — they update themselves and are all off until you turn them on. For vanilla DoW2 without the launcher there are packages below.

Elite (launcher) ↓Vanilla DoW2 ↓
These are prototypes made by a community member, not by the Elite team. Use them at your own risk. If the game crashes, press “Send logs” in the launcher before starting the game again.

In the launcher (Elite)

Launcher → “Add-ons” → tick the ones you want. Each add-on is a separate file: switched off, it is not loaded at all.

Automatch mmfind-0.32 test

One-button matchmaking: Multiplayer → Find a Game → Start Searching. 1v1 / 2v2 / 3v3 and your maps in Map Preferences; “Watch a match” joins the next game as an observer. The game is created and joined for you; Cancel dissolves the found match for everyone.

Every player in the match needs it. Queue and live matches: ladder

Our game servers relay-14 test

Matches go through our servers (Poland, Germany or Moscow) instead of Relic's in the USA: much lower delay.

Works when everyone in the match has it on.

Lobby rating 0.10.0 works

Relic rating next to each player in the lobby; on hover — win rate, games and Steam hours. Only shows information, never kicks anyone.

Works alone, in any lobby.

Ratings on the loading screen loadingratings-2 works

The Relic rating after each player's name on the loading screen of an online match.

Match timer matchtimer-5 test

Match time (minutes:seconds of game time) next to the Menu button.

Cooldown seconds cooldowns-2 test

Seconds left on recharging abilities and global abilities, in the middle of the button.

Reverse reverse-4 test

Like Company of Heroes: hold Ctrl and right-click behind your vehicles (or on the minimap) — they back off driving backwards, front armour to the enemy, instead of turning around.

Uses the game's own orders, nothing changes for the other players.

Observer lag fix obsfix-1 test

Spectating a live game no longer rebuilds the whole match info dozens of times per frame — live spectating runs as smoothly as a replay.

Connection check netprobe-2 experiment

Nothing in the game changes: 15 s after the game starts it checks for 1–2 minutes in the background what gets through from your computer to our servers (TCP and UDP on several ports, ~5 MB) and sends the result to us by itself.

Pure experiment, at your own risk. Test alone: tick it, start the game, wait 2 minutes in the menu. Helps us make our servers work where the connection gets cut on the way (Russia, Ukraine).

For vanilla DoW2 (no launcher)

Unpack into the game folder next to DOW2.exe: you get winmm.dll and the dow2-patches folder. winmm.dll is a small loader: it loads every .dll from dow2-patches, so the packages can be combined — if you already have it, copy only the new .dll into dow2-patches. Game files are not changed; to remove, delete winmm.dll and the folder.

Automatch v2.0 MMFind 0.45 + OwnLobby 2 test

Find a Game works again in vanilla: Multiplayer → Find a Game → Start Searching finds a 1v1 / 2v2 / 3v3 opponent by rating, the match starts by itself within seconds. The search runs on our lobby server, the match through our game server; ordinary Custom Games lobbies are not affected. If it can't run, Find a Game says why.

Only vanilla players with this package are matched (their own queue, never mixed with Elite), so your opponents need it too. If your connection to Europe gets cut (Russia), add RelayForward. Queue and matches: ladder

Online games through our nearest server RelayForward 21 test

Every Relic online game (Custom Games, any lobby) goes through our server nearest to you — Poland, Germany or Moscow — which passes it on to Relic. For those whose way to Relic's servers is cut or slow (Russia: DPI / blocking); Moscow goes on to Relic through Germany. If our servers can't be reached, the game goes straight to Relic, as without the patch.

The same game by another road: no desync, your opponents don't need it. If you still have Relay.dll from an old package, delete it. Turn off: delete RelayForward.dll. Log: dow2-patches\Relay.log.

Jumps don't break on obstacles JumpRetry 2 works

For the Autarch and other jumpers: if, on the way to the jump point, he hopped over an obstacle by himself and that broke the jump order, the order is repeated right after the landing (up to 3 times). Any new order you give the squad cancels the repeat.

The repeat is the game's own order, no desync; your opponents don't need it. Turn off: Enabled=0 in JumpRetry.ini.
Download .zip

Don't drop out of the match when Steam goes away SteamKeep 2 test

When Steam loses its connection during a match, the game leaves the match and often closes. With this the match is played to the end, however long Steam is away; Steam's request to close the game waits until the match is over. Your own Alt+F4, Quit and the close button work as usual.

No desync, your opponents don't need it. Settings: SteamKeep.ini.
